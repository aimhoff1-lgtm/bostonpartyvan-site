Questions about small-group transportation

Do you provide Boston transportation for 10 people?

Yes. A group of 10 is an excellent fit for Boston Party Van. The private Mercedes-Benz Sprinter seats up to 14 passengers and includes a dedicated driver.

Is Boston Party Van a traditional party bus?

Boston Party Van uses a private Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 2500 High Roof rather than a full-size traditional party bus. It is a right-sized option for groups of up to 14 who want to ride together with a dedicated driver.

How many passengers can ride?

The Mercedes-Benz Sprinter seats groups of up to 14 passengers. It is a practical fit for groups of 6, 8, 10, 12, or 14 people.

Does the service include a driver?

Yes. Every trip includes a dedicated driver and route coordination from the first pickup through the final drop-off.

What kinds of Boston trips are a good fit?

Common trips include TD Garden, Fenway Park, Gillette Stadium, concerts, corporate outings, weddings, restaurant stops, bachelor and bachelorette outings, and custom nights out.

Is there a minimum booking?

Yes. The current minimum is three hours for local bookings.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is personalized around the trip date, route, number of stops, group size, timing, and return plan.

Tell us where your group is going

Send your date, group size, pickup location, destination, and timing. We will build a personalized quote around the actual route.
