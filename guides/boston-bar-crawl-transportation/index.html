Quick answers

Do we need to know every stop before requesting a quote?

No. A few preferences, a start area, and your desired finish are enough to begin shaping the route.

Can we adjust the route during the night?

Small adjustments can be discussed with the driver, though the smoothest nights start with a clear core plan.

Will everyone stay together between stops?

That is the point of the route: one group, one driver, and one coordinated return rather than a rideshare scramble.
