Your holiday party starts with the ride

A company celebration, a Christmas dinner with friends, or a family night out should not require a convoy of cars. Boston Party Van keeps your group together in one private, high-roof Sprinter. Andrew plans the pickup, stop sequence, and return around your actual event.

We are available now for holiday transportation from Greater Boston, including Boston, Cambridge, Somerville, Newton, and nearby communities. The van has 14 passenger seats plus a separate driver seat. Request your date and itinerary for personalized pricing; sending an inquiry does not confirm a reservation.

Office and corporate holiday parties

Arrange one office pickup, a hotel departure, or a planned neighborhood pickup loop before your team dinner or company party. Share the venue address, event start time, and when everyone needs to be back. We can plan a round trip or a one-way transfer if your group has a separate return arrangement.

For an evening with several stops, include the restaurant, party venue, and final drop-off location in your request. A clear schedule helps us allow time for loading, traffic, and the end-of-night meeting point.

Christmas parties, dinners, and celebrations

Bring your friends or family together for a Christmas party without dividing the group among several cars. Plan a restaurant dinner, a holiday performance, a private gathering, or a festive night across Boston. Your driver handles the transportation while everyone enjoys the occasion.

Christmas-light displays can be included as a planned stop before or after dinner. Tell us which displays you want to see and any timed admission you have booked. Admission, show tickets, and restaurant reservations are separate from transportation.

Frequently asked questions

How many people can ride to our holiday party?

The Sprinter seats up to 14 passengers plus a separate driver. Share your exact passenger count and any luggage or equipment so we can confirm your plan.

Can you pick up our group at an office or hotel?

Yes. We plan office, hotel, and other Greater Boston pickups. Include every pickup address and the event start time in your inquiry.

Can we add Christmas lights or a dinner stop?

Yes. Include your desired displays, restaurant, stop sequence, and timing so the quote covers the full evening. Tickets and reservations are separate.

Are you available for Christmas and New Year's Eve?

We are unavailable December 25, 2026 through January 4, 2027, inclusive. Holiday-party inquiries are welcome for dates outside that period, with exact availability confirmed before booking.

Can we request a last-minute holiday ride?

Yes. We accept immediate and short-notice inquiries for dates outside the holiday closure. Andrew will review your route and confirm availability; there is no 30-day notice requirement.
