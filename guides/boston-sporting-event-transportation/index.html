Private transportation to Gillette Stadium

Planning a Patriots game or another Foxborough event? Request a private ride for up to 14 passengers from Boston, Somerville, Cambridge, or another Greater Boston pickup. Include your event date, kickoff or show time, and whether everyone is returning together. A dinner or bar stop can be included in the itinerary when the timing allows.

Your service window includes the journey to the stadium, any reserved waiting time, and the return to your final destination. The quote confirms transportation arrangements, parking charges, stops, and any extra-time terms before you book. Event tickets are separate.

Parking and your post-game pickup

Stadium access depends on the vehicle and event. Gillette requires limos and town cars to park and wait rather than simply drop off and leave; oversized-vehicle parking is in Lot 52. We confirm the applicable arrangement for your trip instead of promising a stadium-gate drop-off. Allow time to walk to the entrance and meet the driver afterward. Check Gillette's official transportation rules.

For an evening game, share your desired arrival time and all pre-game stops. Plan the return around the end of the game and the agreed service window, rather than assuming a guaranteed final whistle or departure time.
