Quick answers

Are alcohol-free trips welcome?

Absolutely. Family, school, camp, and community itineraries are a natural fit for a private group ride.

Can we include more than one stop?

Yes. Share the order of your stops and any fixed arrival times so the route can be paced sensibly.

How many guests can ride?

The Sprinter accommodates up to 14 guests. Tell us about the group so we can confirm the right setup.
